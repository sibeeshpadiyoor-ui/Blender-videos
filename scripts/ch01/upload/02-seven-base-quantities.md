Why Only Seven Base Quantities? | Fundamentals of Physics, Ch 1 (1-1)

**Description**
Speed. Force. Energy. Pressure. Physics has hundreds of quantities, so do we need a separate standard for each one? No. In 1971 the world agreed on just seven base quantities, and every other unit is built from them.

In this video of Fundamentals of Physics, Chapter 1 (Measurement), section 1-1, we meet the seven base quantities and their units, and see how speed (m/s) and power (1 W = 1 kg·m²/s³) are built from them.

You will learn:
- what a base quantity is
- the seven base quantities and their SI units
- how other units are built from the base units
- why only a few standards are needed
