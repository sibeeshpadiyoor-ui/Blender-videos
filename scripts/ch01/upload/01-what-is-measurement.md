**Description**
"This table is two long." Two what? A number on its own tells us nothing. To measure means to compare something with an agreed unit, and that is why every measurement needs a number and a unit.

In this first video of Physics, One Idea at a Time we see why measuring is comparing, why hand spans give different answers for the same table, and why the whole world agrees on one standard for each unit.

You will learn:
- what it means to measure
- what a unit is, and why we need an agreed standard
- why a good standard must never change and be available to everyone
- measurement = number × unit
